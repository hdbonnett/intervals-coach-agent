Copy to athlete/profile.md in your instance directory and fill in. It is loaded into every
conversation, so keep it to stable facts; short bullets work best. Delete what doesn't apply.

## Goals
- Main goal and target event(s), with dates.
- Secondary goals (e.g. climbing, longer events).

## Constraints and preferences
- Age, or "masters athlete", if you want recovery weighted more heavily.
- Weekly and daily time available; days that are always short or unavailable.
- Rest days per week you want protected.
- Core metrics (power/TSS, heart rate, pace) and equipment (smart trainer, power meter).
- Preferred test protocols (ramp test, 20-min test, submaximal fatigue test).
- Whether the assistant must ask before writing anything to your calendar.

## Context
- What wellness data you log (sleep, HRV, resting HR, subjective scores).
- Past issues worth remembering (heat, fuelling, injuries).
- Regular group rides or routes, with any notes on terrain or wind.
- Other training (strength, mobility).
